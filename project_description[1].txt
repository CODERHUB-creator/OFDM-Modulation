TITLE: OFDM Modulation Over Two Antennas

AIM:
To design and simulate an OFDM communication system using two transmit antennas and two receive antennas in MATLAB and analyze its BER performance.

SOFTWARE:
MATLAB

MODULATION:
QPSK

OFDM:
64 subcarriers with a 16-sample cyclic prefix.

MIMO:
2 transmit antennas and 2 receive antennas (2x2 MIMO).

CHANNEL:
Independent Rayleigh fading links with AWGN.

RECEIVER:
FFT-based OFDM demodulation followed by Zero-Forcing MIMO equalization and QPSK demodulation.

OUTPUTS:
1. Two-antenna OFDM waveform
2. MIMO channel matrix
3. BER versus SNR curve
4. QPSK constellation

BASIC PRINCIPLE:
OFDM divides a high-rate data stream into many lower-rate parallel streams. Each stream is transmitted on an orthogonal subcarrier. The IFFT converts frequency-domain data into a time-domain OFDM signal. A cyclic prefix is added to reduce inter-symbol interference. Two antennas are used at the transmitter and receiver to form a 2x2 MIMO system. At the receiver, the cyclic prefix is removed, FFT is applied, and the MIMO channel is equalized using a Zero-Forcing detector.
